# A-Gent Content Agent — Spec v1

## What It Does
Generates weekly content for B2B SaaS clients: LinkedIn posts, blog posts, and email sequences. Client approves all output before publishing.

## Inputs
- Product description (1 paragraph)
- Target ICP (ideal customer profile)
- 3-5 topics they want to be known for
- Tone: "like a smart founder" or reference URL

## Weekly Outputs
1. **2 LinkedIn posts** (150-300 words, punchy hook, call to action)
2. **1 blog post** (600-800 words, SEO-friendly, based on their product/industry)
3. **1 email sequence** (3 emails: hook, value prop, CTA)

## Workflow
```
Client Brief (dashboard) → Agent generates → Dashboard approval queue → Client approves/edits → Publish
```

## Content Strategy Framework

### Topics (per client, 3-5):
- Pain point they solve for customers
- Industry trend their product addresses
- "What we learned" (founder voice)
- "How to" advice their product enables
- Counter-intuitive take on their industry

### Tone Guide:
- Write like a sharp founder, not a marketing team
- No corporate speak, no "leverage synergies"
- Short sentences. Bold statements. Real examples.
- Questions that make readers think

### LinkedIn Post Formula:
```
HOOK (first 3 lines must stop the scroll)
↓
BODY (2-4 short paragraphs, 1 insight each)
↓
CTA (engagement prompt, not "learn more")
```

### Blog Post Formula:
```
HEADLINE (question or bold claim)
↓
INTRO (problem statement, 3 sentences)
↓
3-4 SUBHEADINGS (practical takeaways)
↓
CLIFFHANGER CTA (invite to comment or DM)
```

### Email Sequence Formula:
```
Email 1: Counter-intuitive insight (hook)
Email 2: How their problem actually works  
Email 3: Offer / demo invite
```
