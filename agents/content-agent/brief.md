# A-Gent Client Onboarding Brief

## 5 Minutes to Better Content

Answer these 5 questions and we'll build your first week of content:

---

### 1. What do you sell?
(1-3 sentences — what problem do you solve for customers?)

### 2. Who do you sell it to?
(What's your ideal customer? Company size, industry, role of buyer)

### 3. What topics do you want to be known for?
(List 3-5 — e.g., "how to reduce churn," "sales forecasting," "B2B pricing strategy")

### 4. What's your target ICP on LinkedIn?
(Who should be reading your posts? Give me 2-3 example titles or companies)

### 5. Tone reference
(Choose one or mix)
- [ ] Like a sharp startup founder — direct, opinionated, no fluff
- [ ] Like a knowledgeable peer — helpful, practical, collaborative  
- [ ] Like a bold industry rebel — provocative takes, challenges norms
- [ ] Reference: [paste a LinkedIn post URL you like]

---

## What happens next:
1. We research your industry and competitors
2. Agent generates your first week's content (2 LinkedIn posts + 1 blog post + email sequence)
3. You review in your dashboard — approve or request changes
4. We publish and track performance

## Time commitment from you:
- 10 minutes to fill this brief
- 15 minutes to review each week's content
- That's it. We handle the rest.
